## Visual Style Profile
- **Art Style**: Retro 2-D pixel-art with bold color blocks and limited shading, emulating classic arcade sprites while maintaining cinematic composition
- **Color Palette**: Muted teal, deep cobalt, warm amber, iron gray, and parchment beige for medieval tones, contrasted with neon cyan highlights for modern tech
- **Lighting**: Hybrid lighting: warm tungsten key for medieval interiors, cool 5600K daylight rim for modern equipment, with soft ambient fill and dramatic rim highlights
- **Era & Setting**: Early 13th century European-inspired medieval kingdom intersected with a near-future high‑tech military armory
- **Lens & Film Stock**: Shot on 35mm prime lenses with fine grain film emulation, selective shallow depth of field for intimacy

## Character Continuity Sheet
- **PixelSoldier**: PixelSoldier has high cheekbones, an angular jawline, olive‑toned skin, and short cropped dark hair rendered in pixel blocks. He wears a pixelated chest plate of muted teal with brushed metal edge, dark pixelated combat trousers, reinforced black pixel boots, and a utility belt of dark gray pixel pouches. The rifle is a retro arcade‑style pixel weapon slung across his chest, its barrel matte black with teal accent LEDs. All fabrics show subtle pixel‑grid texture, seams slightly frayed, and metal surfaces display a faint brushed‑steel patina.

## Location Continuity Sheet
- **CastleCourtyard**: CastleCourtyard is a stone‑paved open yard surrounded by weathered gray limestone walls, torch‑lit arches, and weather‑worn banners of deep crimson. The ground is uneven cobblestone with occasional moss patches, and a central well of dark stone reflects flickering torchlight. Ambient dust motes drift in the low‑angle light, while distant castle towers loom under a cloudy amber sky.

## Complete Narrator Script

> One elite soldier. The most advanced military equipment of our time. And an entire medieval kingdom that has never seen anything like him. What if he was sent 800 years into the past, armed with everything he needed to survive a modern battlefield, but forced to fight in a world where his ammunition could never be replaced? A single rifle could stop a charging army. His body armor could turn a knight's blade. But he has one problem: he cannot bring the modern world with him. Every bullet he fires brings him closer to running out. Every wound risks becoming his last. And every battle makes him a greater target for kings who would rather own him than let him live. In a world of swords, castles, and endless wars, how long could one modern soldier survive before the medieval era takes everything he has? Would he become the greatest warrior in history, or would the past finally defeat the future?
