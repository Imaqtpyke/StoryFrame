# Production Storyboard & Shot List

**Platform**: TikTok (9:16 Vertical)
**Generation Mode**: Text to Image
**Estimated Duration**: ~20s
**Total Scenes**: 2 | **Total Visual Beats**: 8

## Visual Style Profile
- **Art Style**: Bold stylized art blending clean 2D stickman animation, expressive anime character faces, soft thumbprinted claymation surfaces, and thick visible oil-painting impasto brushstrokes in a handcrafted storybook atmosphere
- **Color Palette**: Warm gallery ochre, terracotta, crimson, cobalt blue, and sunlit gold contrasting against stormy slate-indigo and stark gesso canvas white
- **Lighting**: Warm 3200K tungsten gallery spotlights paired with crisp 5200K skylight fill and dramatic high-contrast rim separation
- **Era & Setting**: Whimsical timeless storybook metropolis and grand vaulted art museum built from hand-painted oil canvases and varnished parquet galleries
- **Lens & Film Stock**: Shot on cinema prime lenses, tactile painted canvas grain and handcrafted matte stop-motion depth

## Character Continuity Sheet
- **Ink**: Simple jet-black stickman with a round matte-black head, large expressive white eyes, a long crimson knit scarf draped around his neck, bare stick feet, and a wooden-handled paintbrush staff with paint-dipped bristles
- **Claymation Cat**: Small lumpy terracotta-and-cream claymation cat companion with soft thumbprint sculpting textures, oversized round cartoon eyes, sculpted whiskers, stubby paws, and a curled clay tail
- **Anime-Style Inventor**: Young anime-style inventor with spiky chestnut hair, brass workshop goggles perched on her forehead, a smudge-stained canvas apron over a rolled-sleeve tunic, leather tool satchel, and laced leather boots
- **Animated Drawings**: Flat charcoal-and-gouache sketch creatures with hand-drawn ink outlines and fluttering paper-thin edges

## Location Continuity Sheet
- **Brushwick City**: Hand-painted storybook metropolis with crooked oil-painted rooftops, impasto cobblestone avenues, and giant wooden gallery easels framing the skyline
- **Brushwick Art Museum**: Vaulted grand art gallery featuring towering ornate gilded picture frames, stretched linen canvases, marble columns, and warm varnished parquet floorboards

## Complete Narrator Script

> In the hand-painted city of Brushwick, a stickman artist named Ink discovers his animated drawings are escaping the canvas. With a claymation cat and an anime-style inventor, Ink must paint a doorway home before the museum’s oil-painted storm erases every color from their world.
