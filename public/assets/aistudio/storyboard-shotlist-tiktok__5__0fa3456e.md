# Production Storyboard & Shot List

**Platform**: TikTok (9:16 Vertical)
**Generation Mode**: Text to Image
**Estimated Duration**: ~21s
**Total Scenes**: 3 | **Total Visual Beats**: 11

## Visual Style Profile
- **Art Style**: Cinematic cyberpunk realism with gritty wet-surface textures, mixed analog-digital hardware props, dense futuristic urban architecture, and a grounded, hopeful sci-fi tone
- **Color Palette**: Deep charcoal and wet obsidian shadows, glowing cyan circuitry, vibrant neon magenta accents, brushed chrome, and a late shift to warm golden dawn amber
- **Lighting**: High-contrast neon chiaroscuro with 5200K to 5800K teal and magenta specular reflections on rain-slick asphalt, transitioning to 3200K warm golden sunrise rim light at the climax
- **Era & Setting**: Near-future dystopian Neo-Kaido, a vertically stratified metropolis of brutalist skyscrapers, holographic billboards, rain-soaked neon alleys, and industrial tenement rooftops
- **Lens & Film Stock**: Shot on 35mm anamorphic-style spherical prime lenses, subtle natural film grain, crisp highlight halation

## Character Continuity Sheet
- **Mira Vale**: A lean woman in her mid-twenties with olive skin, sharp jawline, and asymmetrical dark undercut hair, wearing a weathered black techwear jacket traced with glowing cyan circuitry seams, a matte-black lower-face half-mask visor with a faint teal status indicator, dark utility cargo trousers, and scuffed black combat boots.
- **Chrome-Faced Enforcers**: Tall armored tactical operatives wearing seamless polished liquid-chrome featureless faceplates, heavy matte-carbon trench coats with glowing magenta shoulder optics, dark tactical trousers, and thick black polymer boots.

## Location Continuity Sheet
- **Neo-Kaido Neon Streets**: A cavernous, rain-drenched futuristic street canyon lined with towering brutalist skyscrapers, glowing holographic kanji signage, steaming sidewalk grates, tangled overhead cables, and mirror-wet black asphalt reflecting teal and magenta light.
- **Rooftop Broadcast Overlook**: A windswept, rain-washed concrete tenement rooftop high above the tiered city districts, cluttered with wet steel air vents, puddle-filled concrete slabs, rusted guardrails, and a towering communication relay console.

## Complete Narrator Script

> In Neo-Kaido, courier Mira Vale smuggles a stolen memory-chip through rain-soaked neon streets. Hunted by chrome-faced enforcers, she discovers it holds the city’s erased history, and her own. At dawn, Mira must choose: sell the truth or broadcast it, igniting a revolution across the hyphenated districts.
