# Production Storyboard & Shot List

**Platform**: TikTok (9:16 Vertical)
**Generation Mode**: Text to Image
**Estimated Duration**: ~19s
**Total Scenes**: 4 | **Total Visual Beats**: 12

## Visual Style Profile
- **Art Style**: Hyper-realistic cinematic digital painting with tactile gritty textures, emphasizing rough salt-crusted metal and coarse wool fabric rendering.
- **Color Palette**: Deep obsidian blacks, crashing sea-foam greys, stormy slate blues, and brilliant lighthouse lantern gold.
- **Lighting**: High-contrast chiaroscuro with aggressive directional beams from the rotating lantern, cutting through dense gloom with deep shadows.
- **Era & Setting**: Mid-20th century, isolated storm-battered rocky islet, late night during a violent hurricane.
- **Lens & Film Stock**: 35mm anamorphic lens with a subtle heavy grain, desaturated stocks with warm highlights.

## Character Continuity Sheet
- **The Keeper**: Seventy-year-old male, deep facial wrinkles, salt-and-pepper stubble, pale blue eyes, heavy oversized dark wool pea coat, oil-stained heavy canvas trousers, worn-through leather boots

## Location Continuity Sheet
- **Lighthouse Interior**: Spiral iron staircase with heavy rust patina, reinforced circular glass lantern room, brass lantern housing, central rotating fresnel lens, scuffed wooden floorboards.

## Complete Narrator Script

> The old lighthouse keeper climbed the rusted stairs for the final time. Outside, the storm raged against the glass. He lit the lamp, watching the beam cut through the darkness like a golden blade. His watch was over, but the light would guide the lost souls home forever tonight.
