# Production Storyboard & Shot List

**Platform**: TikTok (9:16 Vertical)
**Generation Mode**: Text to Image
**Estimated Duration**: ~9s
**Total Scenes**: 1 | **Total Visual Beats**: 6

## Visual Style Profile
- **Art Style**: minimalist stickman vector art with thick black outlines, flat digital shading, and high contrast hand-drawn ink aesthetics.
- **Color Palette**: deep abyss black, bioluminescent cyan, radioactive neon green, and electric void purple.
- **Lighting**: sharp high-contrast directional bioluminescence emanating from city structures and submersible searchlights.
- **Era & Setting**: the present day, 11,000 meters deep within the Mariana Trench, encased in high-pressure dark ocean water.
- **Lens & Film Stock**: wide-angle distorted fisheye lens simulation, 8mm digital grain texture.

## Character Continuity Sheet
- **Biologist A**: stickman figure, circle head, thin straight torso and limbs, wearing a simple round white helmet bubble
- **Biologist B**: stickman figure, circle head, thin straight torso and limbs, wearing a simple round white helmet bubble with a single dash antenna
- **Submersible**: minimalist rounded rectangular capsule with a large circular viewport and two stick-thin mechanical arm probes

## Location Continuity Sheet
- **Mariana Trench Abyssal Plain**: featureless dark void with scattered jagged geometric stick-figure rock formations.
- **Illuminated Metropolis**: symmetrical grid of vertical lines and concentric circles glowing in cyan, resembling an ancient motherboard architecture fused with organic coral patterns.

## Complete Narrator Script

> Two deep-sea marine biologists in a research submersible encounter an illuminated underwater metropolis buried inside the Mariana Trench that responds to sonar pulses with musical harmonics.
