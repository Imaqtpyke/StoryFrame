# Production Storyboard & Shot List

**Platform**: TikTok (9:16 Vertical)
**Generation Mode**: Text to Video (~4s clips)
**Estimated Duration**: ~32s
**Total Scenes**: 8 | **Total Visual Beats**: 15

## Visual Style Profile
- **Art Style**: handcrafted miniature world built from soft-matte clay, weathered oak, and heavy ivory cardstock, lit with warm studio key lights and deep directional shadows. Contracts are massive accordion-folded paper structures with debossed black lettering, red ink signatures, and raised crimson wax seals that physically crack, shift, and bind characters.
- **Color Palette**: warm earth tones with muted clay reds, deep oak browns, ivory highlights, and soft amber lighting
- **Lighting**: warm tungsten key light at 3200K with soft fill at 5600K, creating gentle shadows and rim highlights
- **Era & Setting**: contemporary suburban setting with a minimalist home office and a classic bank interior, rendered in miniature scale
- **Lens & Film Stock**: Shot on 35mm anamorphic prime lens, fine film grain, high dynamic range

## Character Continuity Sheet
- **Homeowner**: Adult, medium build, short textured dark hair, neutral facial features, wearing a plain gray sweater, blue jeans, and white sneakers. Stance is natural, feet planted firmly on the miniature floor.

## Location Continuity Sheet
- **Home Office**: A compact desk made of weathered oak, a clay laptop, a stack of massive accordion-folded contract papers, soft-matte clay wall background, warm studio lighting.
- **Bank Interior**: Miniature marble counter, clay bank vault door, rows of tiny ledger books, deep directional shadows, warm amber ambience.
