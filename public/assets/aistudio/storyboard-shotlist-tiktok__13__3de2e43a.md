## Visual Style Profile
- **Art Style**: Frost , a small snow-spirit with translucent blue-white fur, glowing pale eyes, crystalline antlers, and drifting frost trailing from its paws. Style: cozy hand-painted fantasy animation with soft watercolor textures, snowy pine forests, warm lantern glow against cool blue winter light, expressive animal-like movement, and gentle golden flashbacks that contrast with the crisp present-day blizzard.
- **Color Palette**: Harmonious color grading matching narrative tone
- **Lighting**: Cinematic three-point lighting with soft rim fill
- **Era & Setting**: Contemporary atmospheric setting
- **Lens & Film Stock**: Shot on 35mm prime lens, fine film grain

## Complete Narrator Script

> In the mountain village of Everfrost, young healer Sable finds an injured snow-spirit named Frost. Flashbacks reveal Sable's late mother teaching her to listen to the forest. As an ancient blizzard returns, Sable and Frost must reach the mountain shrine, but saving the spirit may mean letting go of her mother's memory.
