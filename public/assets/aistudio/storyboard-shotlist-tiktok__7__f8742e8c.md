# Production Storyboard & Shot List

**Platform**: TikTok (9:16 Vertical)
**Generation Mode**: Text to Image
**Estimated Duration**: ~22.5s
**Total Scenes**: 3 | **Total Visual Beats**: 10

## Visual Style Profile
- **Art Style**: Psychological horror realism with tactile 35mm cinematic grain, dust-choked Victorian textures, distorted glass reflections, and shallow focus
- **Color Palette**: Desaturated charcoal grays, warm flickering amber lantern gold, tarnished brass, and cold nocturnal moonlight blue
- **Lighting**: Dim 2700K amber oil lantern key light clashing against 5600K cold blue moonlight with deep high-contrast shadows
- **Era & Setting**: Contemporary nocturnal psychological horror set inside an inherited, derelict Victorian house filled with antique furniture and cracked mirrors
- **Lens & Film Stock**: Shot on 35mm and 85mm anamorphic-inspired prime lenses, fine silver-halide film grain

## Character Continuity Sheet
- **Elara**: A pale, gaunt young woman in her late twenties with dark under-eye shadows, hollow cheekbones, and long tousled dark hair with a heavy lock falling over her left eye, wearing an oversized loose charcoal knit sweater, worn dark trousers, and scuffed dark leather boots.
- **TheWomanBehindHer**: A gaunt older woman with sunken cheeks, pale ashen skin, and long tangled silver-streaked dark hair, wearing a faded dusty charcoal nightgown with bare pale feet.

## Location Continuity Sheet
- **LateMothersHouseParlor**: A cramped, derelict Victorian parlor-bedroom inside an inherited house, featuring peeling dark floral wallpaper, warped dark oak floorboards coated in dust, antique furniture draped in white linen sheets, a tall tarnished brass-framed wall mirror with fine hairline cracks, and a shadowy wrought-iron bed covered in moth-eaten gray blankets in the rear corner.

## Complete Narrator Script

> Insomniac archivist Elara finds a mirror in her late mother’s house that reflects a version of her moving seconds ahead. Each night, the reflection gets closer. When Elara finally confronts it, she realizes the mirror has been showing her, and the woman sleeping behind her has been waiting.
