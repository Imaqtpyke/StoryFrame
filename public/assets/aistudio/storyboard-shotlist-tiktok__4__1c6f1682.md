# Production Storyboard & Shot List

**Platform**: TikTok (9:16 Vertical)
**Generation Mode**: Text to Image
**Estimated Duration**: ~36s
**Total Scenes**: 7 | **Total Visual Beats**: 18

## Visual Style Profile
- **Art Style**: Atmospheric 2.5D ink-illustrated Neo-Noir animation featuring stylized black-ink stickman characters with round white heads and expressive ink line-art faces against richly textured retro-cyberpunk urban environments and tactile silver-gelatin darkroom photography
- **Color Palette**: Deep rainy asphalt slate and charcoal shadows contrasted with darkroom crimson safelight, warm sodium streetlamp amber, neon stark white accents, weathered khaki, transit orange, and stark monochrome black-and-white silver-gelatin film prints
- **Lighting**: High-contrast nocturnal neo-noir chiaroscuro with rain-slicked specular neon reflections, intense crimson darkroom safelight glow, and harsh directional headlight beams cutting through mist
- **Era & Setting**: 1980s Neo-Seoul, rain-drenched retro-futuristic Korean metropolis filled with vertical Hangul neon signs, concrete alleyways, analog transit buses, mechanical 35mm rangefinders, and basement photo darkrooms
- **Lens & Film Stock**: Shot on vintage 35mm spherical prime lenses with tactile 400-speed silver-gelatin film grain and subtle halation

## Character Continuity Sheet
- **Photographer**: Slender black-ink stickman figure with a crisp round white head and expressive ink line-art brows and mouth, wearing a boxy belted khaki trench coat with upturned collar, charcoal trousers, black laced leather boots, and a battered silver 35mm rangefinder camera on a frayed brown leather strap
- **StrayChild**: Tiny black-ink stickman child with a round white head and expressive ink line-art features, wearing an oversized glossy yellow hooded raincoat, rolled-up dark shorts, and small red rubber rain boots

## Location Continuity Sheet
- **NeoSeoulCrosswalk**: Rain-drenched 1980s Neo-Seoul four-lane asphalt intersection with faded white zebra crosswalk stripes, glowing vertical Hangul neon signs, concrete utility poles, metal streetlamps, and a corner wooden newsstand
- **BasementDarkroom**: Cramped cinderblock basement photo darkroom lit by an overhead crimson safelight, featuring hanging negative film strips, stainless-steel chemical trays on a wet concrete counter, and wooden drying racks

## Complete Narrator Script

> In 1980s Neo-Seoul, a young street photographer shoots an empty crosswalk at midnight. Inside his basement darkroom, he drops the film into developer, and watches shapes emerge. The black-and-white print shows a smashed city bus, a headline board, and his own fallen camera. He checks his wristwatch: twelve o'clock sharp. He has ten minutes before it happens. Sprinting back to the empty crosswalk, he spots an orange bus speeding through red mist. He tackles a stray child off the street, drops his camera, and rolls onto the curb. The bus misses them both, leaving only a shattered lens and an impossible second chance.
