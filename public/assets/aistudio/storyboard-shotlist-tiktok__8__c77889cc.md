# Production Storyboard & Shot List

**Platform**: TikTok (9:16 Vertical)
**Generation Mode**: Text to Image
**Estimated Duration**: ~13.6s
**Total Scenes**: 3 | **Total Visual Beats**: 13

## Visual Style Profile
- **Art Style**: High-contrast 2D stickman figures rendered with coarse, hand-drawn ink-wash textures, utilizing cross-hatched shadows and jagged, charcoal-brushed edges.
- **Color Palette**: Deep obsidian blacks, stark paper-white backgrounds, and weathered sepia-toned parchment highlights.
- **Lighting**: Harsh, dramatic rim lighting with high-contrast chiaroscuro, mimicking the stark directional beams of a flashlight cutting through damp, murky darkness.
- **Era & Setting**: A post-apocalyptic coastal ruin, characterized by flooded concrete structures, submerged bookshelves, and pervasive marine decay during a stagnant, foggy low tide.
- **Lens & Film Stock**: Fixed 35mm prime lens perspective with the visual grain and flickering instability of degraded 16mm black-and-white high-contrast film stock.

## Character Continuity Sheet
- **The Scavenger**: A minimalist stickman silhouette with a hunched posture, wearing a tattered wide-brimmed hat and a heavy, sodden hooded cloak draped loosely over a thin, wiry frame

## Location Continuity Sheet
- **The Submerged Library**: A ruined interior featuring decaying stone pillars, skeletal wooden bookshelf frames encrusted with jagged barnacles, and shallow standing water reflecting faint, erratic light sources.

## Complete Narrator Script

> A lone scavenger digs through the flooded ruins of an ancient coastal library, hunting for surviving paper books. Beneath barnacles and salt crust, he pulls out a sealed leather journal. When he opens it, fresh dry pages rustle in the damp air, preserving words from a world lost to water.
