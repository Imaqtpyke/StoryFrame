# Production Storyboard & Shot List

**Platform**: TikTok (9:16 Vertical)
**Generation Mode**: Text to Image
**Estimated Duration**: ~31.5s
**Total Scenes**: 7 | **Total Visual Beats**: 30

## Visual Style Profile
- **Art Style**: Low‑polygon PS1‑era 3D rendering with CRT scanlines, VHS grain, dithering, and pixelated textures
- **Color Palette**: Muted teal, deep indigo, cold cyan, amber flicker, desaturated earth tones
- **Lighting**: Dim amber key light from a single flickering fluorescent tube, cold blue rim lighting, heavy shadows, atmospheric fog, scanline glow
- **Era & Setting**: Late 1990s Filipino suburban house at 3 AM, analog horror atmosphere, cramped hallway, tiled floor, wooden doors, family photos
- **Lens & Film Stock**: Shot on 35mm prime lens, fine analog film grain simulation, low‑resolution pixel texture

## Character Continuity Sheet
- **Student**: Student, low‑polygon male college student, high cheekbones, olive skin, short black hair, wearing a plain white short‑sleeve t‑shirt, faded blue denim jeans, and worn canvas sneakers. Hands visible in lower frame; right hand clutching an old Nokia 3310, fingers in a relaxed natural arc, wrist flexed ~20°, thumb resting on middle knuckle. Left hand rests on wooden floor, palm flat. Torso twisted ~10° to screen‑left, hips counter‑angled, weight 55% on left foot, 45% on right, both feet planted shoulder‑width. Neck slightly forward, chin dipped, gaze fixed on phone screen.
- **Mother**: Mother, low‑polygon Filipino woman, high cheekbones, pale sallow skin, dark brown hair pulled back, wearing a simple floral nightgown with faded pink pattern, loose sleeves, thin white slippers. Standing in dim hallway, weight 60% on right foot, left heel slightly lifted, shoulders slumped, torso slightly leaned forward, head tilted down, eyes wide and pale, mouth slightly open. Holds her own Nokia phone in both hands, fingers curled around edges, wrist neutral, gaze fixed on ringing screen.

## Location Continuity Sheet
- **HouseHallway**: Narrow dimly lit hallway of a late‑90s Filipino house, wooden floorboards with worn varnish, walls lined with faded family photos in cheap frames, cracked plaster ceiling, a single flickering fluorescent tube casting cold blue rim light, dust motes drifting in stale air, low‑poly geometry, heavy CRT scanline overlay, faint ambient fog hugging the floor, tiled sections uneven, shadows deepening toward the far end.

## Complete Narrator Script

> College student pa ako noon. Tapos, mga bandang 3 AM. biglang may katok nang katok sa pinto ko, parang may nangyaring kung ano. Pagbukas ko, putla ang mama ko. Pinakita niya sa akin ang phone niya na ring nang ring. Sabi ko, "Anong problema?" "Ba't hindi mo sinasagot?" Sabi niya, "Patay na 'yung may-ari ng number na 'yun." Kakagaling lang nila sa lamay, at kasama sa kabaong ang phone niya at ilang personal na gamit din. Pinapilit niya sa akin na sagutin, pero, ayun nga, walang sumasagot sa kabila, pero hindi rin binababa. Though, ramdam ko na parang may nasa kabila nga.
