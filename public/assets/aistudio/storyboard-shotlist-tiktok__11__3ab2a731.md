# Production Storyboard & Shot List

**Platform**: TikTok (9:16 Vertical)
**Generation Mode**: Text to Image
**Estimated Duration**: ~1m 3s
**Total Scenes**: 5 | **Total Visual Beats**: 14

## Visual Style Profile
- **Art Style**: Handcrafted miniature world built from soft-matte clay, weathered oak, and heavy ivory cardstock, where contracts are massive accordion-folded paper structures with debossed black lettering, red ink signatures, and raised crimson wax seals that physically crack, shift, and bind characters.
- **Color Palette**: Warm ivory cardstock, weathered oak brown, muted slate-blue, charcoal suit gray, and deep crimson wax-seal red.
- **Lighting**: Warm 3200K studio key lights with deep directional shadows and crisp 5000K rim separation.
- **Era & Setting**: Timeless miniature financial office and domestic tabletop world crafted from clay, oak, and heavy paper.
- **Lens & Film Stock**: Shot on 50mm macro prime lens, subtle stop-motion claymation texture

## Character Continuity Sheet
- **The Homeowner**: A handcrafted soft-matte clay miniature figure with short dark brown sculpted hair, warm beige clay skin, wearing a muted slate-blue knit sweater, charcoal trousers, and scuffed brown clay boots
- **The Lender**: A tall, rigid soft-matte clay miniature figure with slicked-back gray sculpted hair, silver-rimmed spectacles, wearing a pinstriped charcoal suit, crisp white collar, crimson tie, and polished black clay dress shoes

## Location Continuity Sheet
- **Miniature Oak Desk**: A vast weathered oak tabletop landscape with visible wood grain, brass lamp base, and towering accordion-folded ivory cardstock loan contracts.
- **Homeowner Clay Cottage**: A cozy handcrafted miniature clay and cardstock house resting on an oak shelf, with warm recessed windows and a tiny oak front door.

## Complete Narrator Script

> If you pay off your mortgage ten years early, your lender might hit you with a massive prepayment penalty fee costing thousands of dollars. When you sign a home loan, the bank calculates decades of guaranteed interest payments to lock in their long-term profit margins. If you work hard, save money, and pay off your remaining balance ahead of schedule, you suddenly cut off the bank's future income stream. To prevent this loss, lenders often hide a prepayment penalty clause deep in your loan contract that triggers the exact moment you clear your debt. This fee can legally force you to hand over up to six months of interest or a fixed percentage of your original principal, effectively punishing you for getting out of debt early unless you explicitly verify your contract has a zero-penalty clause before signing.
