# Production Storyboard & Shot List

**Platform**: TikTok (9:16 Vertical)
**Generation Mode**: Text to Image
**Estimated Duration**: ~19s
**Total Scenes**: 4 | **Total Visual Beats**: 8

## Visual Style Profile
- **Art Style**: 2D minimalist paper cut animation with high contrast flat black ink lines on textured off white cardstock background
- **Color Palette**: ivory paper parchment, stark charcoal black, vibrant crimson paper, raw earth clay brown, and forest pine green
- **Lighting**: sharp 2D directional light casting hard paper shadow edges simulating a layered tabletop diorama
- **Era & Setting**: an eternal storybook dimension comprised of parchment plains and the dense Inkwell Forest during a perpetual paper dusk
- **Lens & Film Stock**: macro lens 35mm equivalent with a subtle grain texture resembling vintage matte stop motion film

## Character Continuity Sheet
- **Pip**: Simple black stickman body, two round white eyes, red rectangular paper scarf, yellow cedar pencil tucked behind his left ear
- **Child Pip**: Simple black stickman body, two round white eyes, crooked crayon smile drawn in messy wax, red paper scarf, small brown bound sketchbook held under his arm
- **Moss**: Handcrafted uneven brown clay creature, lumpy spherical torso, four thick stubby limbs, smooth texture, embedded twig fragments

## Location Continuity Sheet
- **Paper Cut Village**: Flat architectural cutouts of paper houses with serrated edges and jagged accordion folded hills
- **Inkwell Forest**: Dense thicket of tall ink stained paper trees with drooping branches, puddles of pooling black liquid ink, and a central ancient tree with deep gouged bark

## Complete Narrator Script

> In a paper-cut village, stickman Pip finds a torn map leading to the Inkwell Forest. There, he meets Moss, a handcrafted clay creature guarding the last living tree. Flashbacks show Pip drawing Moss as a child. To save the forest, Pip must finish the drawing before the ink dries forever.
