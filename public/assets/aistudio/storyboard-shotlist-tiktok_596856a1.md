## Visual Style Profile
- **Art Style**: Handcrafted miniature stop-motion diorama built from soft-matte sculpted clay, weathered oak, and heavy ivory cardstock, featuring massive accordion-folded paper contracts with debossed black lettering, red ink signatures, and raised crimson wax seals that physically crack, shift, and bind characters.
- **Color Palette**: Warm ivory cardstock, rich weathered oak browns, soft-matte terracotta clay, crimson wax and ink accents, brushed antique brass, and deep charcoal shadows.
- **Lighting**: Warm 3200K studio tungsten key lights paired with subtle 5200K cool rim highlights and deep directional shadows emphasizing matte clay and cardstock textures.
- **Era & Setting**: Timeless miniature financial diorama world combining a cozy cardstock home studio desk and an imposing weathered oak bank hall.
- **Lens & Film Stock**: Shot on miniature macro studio prime lenses with shallow depth of field and subtle organic film grain.

## Character Continuity Sheet
- **Clay Homeowner**: A handcrafted soft-matte clay miniature figure with rounded human proportions, warm terracotta clay skin tone, short sculpted dark brown clay hair, wearing a slate-blue knitted wool miniature sweater, charcoal trousers, and sturdy brown leather clay boots.
- **Clay Bank Lender**: A tall, rigid soft-matte clay miniature figure with sharp angular facial planes, sleek silver-gray sculpted hair, wire-rimmed brass spectacles, wearing a tailored pinstripe charcoal clay suit, crisp ivory shirt, crimson silk tie, and polished black clay dress shoes.

## Location Continuity Sheet
- **Miniature Cardstock Home Studio**: A handcrafted diorama workspace built from weathered oak floorboards and desk planks, ivory cardstock blueprints, carved wooden ledger blocks, and a miniature sculpted clay house lit by warm studio tungsten lamps.
- **Miniature Oak Bank Hall**: An imposing handcrafted diorama interior built from dark weathered oak floorboards, towering fluted oak columns, heavy ivory cardstock ledger shelves, antique brass bank counters, and warm studio desk lamps casting deep directional shadows.

## Complete Narrator Script

> If you pay off your mortgage ten years early, your lender might hit you with a massive prepayment penalty fee costing thousands of dollars. When you sign a home loan, the bank calculates decades of guaranteed interest payments to lock in their long-term profit margins. If you work hard, save money, and pay off your remaining balance ahead of schedule, you suddenly cut off the bank's future income stream. To prevent this loss, lenders often hide a prepayment penalty clause deep in your loan contract that triggers the exact moment you clear your debt. This fee can legally force you to hand over up to six months of interest or a fixed percentage of your original principal, effectively punishing you for getting out of debt early unless you explicitly verify your contract has a zero-penalty clause before signing.
