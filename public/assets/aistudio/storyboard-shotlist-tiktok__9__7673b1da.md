# Production Storyboard & Shot List

**Platform**: TikTok (9:16 Vertical)
**Generation Mode**: Text to Image
**Estimated Duration**: ~21.3s
**Total Scenes**: 3 | **Total Visual Beats**: 9

## Visual Style Profile
- **Art Style**: Cinematic animated realism with warm golden childhood flashbacks, cool blue-gray present-day coastal tones, soft film grain, expressive character acting, and clear visual transitions between past and present
- **Color Palette**: Cool slate blue-gray, desaturated sea-foam, deep navy, and wet charcoal basalt in present-day Harborline; warm amber, honeyed gold, sun-bleached driftwood beige, and vivid yellow in childhood flashbacks
- **Lighting**: Overcast 5600K to 5800K cool coastal twilight contrasted with 3000K warm handheld brass lantern light in the present; rich 3000K golden-hour coastal sunlight in childhood flashbacks
- **Era & Setting**: Present-day Harborline coastal lighthouse headland and pier contrasted with sun-drenched coastal cove flashbacks from twenty years earlier
- **Lens & Film Stock**: Shot on spherical cinema prime lenses (24mm to 100mm), soft 35mm film grain, tactile atmospheric haze

## Character Continuity Sheet
- **Adult Elena**: Adult woman in her mid-thirties with silver-streaked dark hair tied neatly back, weathered fair skin, a buttoned navy waterproof raincoat, dark rubber coastal boots, and a brass storm lantern
- **Child Elena**: Nine-year-old girl with dark hair tucked under a bright yellow rain hat, a yellow knit sweater over sturdy dark trousers, worn coastal sneakers, and a clothbound sketchbook
- **Adult Rowan**: Adult man in his mid-thirties with tousled dark-brown hair, weathered tanned skin, a faded olive-green canvas jacket over a dark wool shirt, worn brown leather boots, and a scarred brass pocket compass
- **Child Rowan**: Nine-year-old boy with messy brown hair, sun-tanned skin, a rolled-sleeve striped cotton shirt, sturdy canvas shorts, worn canvas sneakers, and an unscarred shiny brass pocket compass

## Location Continuity Sheet
- **Harborline Lighthouse**: Towering weathered stone coastal lighthouse perched atop wet dark basalt cliffs in Harborline, featuring peeling whitewashed interior plaster walls, a scarred oak keeper desk, brass storm gauges, and a stone threshold overlooking misty Atlantic swells
- **Harborline Coastal Cove**: Sheltered rocky shoreline and wooden pier below Harborline headland with barnacle-crusted timber pilings, salt-bleached driftwood, shallow tide pools, and sea cliffs

## Complete Narrator Script

> In present-day Harborline, lighthouse keeper Elena finds a message from her childhood friend Rowan, who vanished twenty years ago. As she reads, flashbacks reveal their shared summers and a promise to reunite. Rowan returns changed, but Elena must decide whether their old bond can survive the truth.
