# Production Storyboard & Shot List

**Platform**: TikTok (9:16 Vertical)
**Generation Mode**: Text to Image
**Estimated Duration**: ~9s
**Total Scenes**: 1 | **Total Visual Beats**: 5

## Visual Style Profile
- **Art Style**: 2D minimalist stickman silhouettes with high contrast ink lines on a textured grainy paper background
- **Color Palette**: monochrome palette of deep carbon black, stark off-white paper, and neon-pulse stark white accents
- **Lighting**: hard noir film noir lighting with deep shadows and harsh volumetric light rays hitting smoke
- **Era & Setting**: 1980s Neo-Seoul, a dense rain-slicked urban sprawl filled with holographic advertisements and metal fire escapes
- **Lens & Film Stock**: 35mm anamorphic prime lens profile with simulated Kodak Tri-X 400 grain and subtle vertical scanning lines

## Character Continuity Sheet
- **Joon the Photographer**: stickman figure with a prominent rectangular camera strapped around his neck, wearing a long trench coat and a brimmed fedora
- **The Headlines**: floating 2D sans-serif digital text blocks in a glowing cyan terminal font

## Location Continuity Sheet
- **Joon's Darkroom**: cramped basement filled with hanging film strips, chemical trays, and a single flickering overhead bulb
- **Neo-Seoul Street**: narrow alleyway featuring towering stacks of glowing holographic signs, metallic dumpsters, and rain-puddled asphalt

## Complete Narrator Script

> A young street photographer in 1980s Neo-Seoul develops black-and-white film that unexpectedly captures future headlines ten minutes before they happen.
