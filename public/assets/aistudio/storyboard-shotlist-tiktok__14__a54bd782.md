# Production Storyboard & Shot List

**Platform**: TikTok (9:16 Vertical)
**Generation Mode**: Text to Image
**Estimated Duration**: ~21s
**Total Scenes**: 1 | **Total Visual Beats**: 14

## Visual Style Profile
- **Art Style**: low polygon pixelated 3D with flat shading, limited texture resolution, PS1 style rendering
- **Color Palette**: muted teal sea blues, rusted copper ship metal, coral pinks, deep abyssal navy, lantern amber highlights
- **Lighting**: soft overcast lantern glow with bioluminescent teal shafts, twilight ambience, occasional lightning flashes
- **Era & Setting**: steampunk-inspired floating harbor city of Tidebreak in a perpetual stormy sea, 22nd century alternate Earth
- **Lens & Film Stock**: 35mm f/1.8 prime lens, Kodak Vision3 500T emulation

## Character Continuity Sheet
- **Coral (Adult)**: Human female sailor, mid-20s, tanned skin, short wavy auburn hair, muscular build, weathered navy coat, leather boots, rope belt, no expression
- **Young Coral**: Human girl, ~8 years old, light skin, braids of auburn hair, slight frame, simple linen shirt and shorts, no expression
- **Pearl (Adult)**: Mermaid teen, iridescent teal scales from waist down, long seaweed-green hair, slender torso, shell necklace, no expression
- **Young Pearl**: Mermaid child, ~7 years old, smaller tail, pastel pink scales, short wavy hair, simple pearl-studded headband, no expression

## Location Continuity Sheet
- **Tidebreak Harbor**: Floating platform of weathered wooden planks supported by rusted iron pylons, lanterns hanging from beams, cargo crates, sea mist swirling around, no moving characters
- **Shipwreck Site**: Scattered broken hull fragments, barnacle-covered timbers, rusted cannons, seaweed draping over debris, static arrangement
- **Surface Deck**: Open sky above the city, gulls perched on railings, bright sunlight breaking through clouds, static horizon line
- **Storm Sea**: Dark churning waves, heavy rain sheets, occasional lightning arcs, static storm clouds over the horizon

## Complete Narrator Script

> In the floating harbor city of Tidebreak, sailor Coral discovers that mermaid Pearl has been stealing pieces of old shipwrecks. Flashbacks reveal Coral and Pearl as childhood friends, separated by a storm. As Tidebreak begins to sink, Coral must trust Pearl's secret plan, even if it means leaving the surface forever.
