# Production Storyboard & Shot List

**Platform**: TikTok (9:16 Vertical)
**Generation Mode**: Text to Image
**Estimated Duration**: ~29.5s
**Total Scenes**: 7 | **Total Visual Beats**: 14

## Visual Style Profile
- **Art Style**: Junji Ito-inspired black and white manga illustration, heavy solid black ink shadows, detailed fine linework, cross-hatching and stippling, no gray gradients, high contrast
- **Color Palette**: Monochrome high contrast black and white, deep blacks with stark white highlights
- **Lighting**: Dim candlelit key light at 3200K warm tungsten, with cold 5400K rim light for contrast, deep chiaroscuro shadows
- **Era & Setting**: Contemporary interior, cramped dark bedroom with an ornate antique ebony-framed mirror, Victorian gothic ambience
- **Lens & Film Stock**: Shot on 35mm black-white film grain, prime lenses with shallow depth of field

## Character Continuity Sheet
- **Protagonist**: young person with short black hair, pallid skin, thin black turtleneck sweater, dark jeans, barefoot, eyes wide, neutral expression

## Location Continuity Sheet
- **DarkRoom**: cramped dim bedroom, cracked wooden floor, walls covered in ink-heavy cross-hatching, single antique ebony-framed ornate mirror on the far wall, single candle providing warm flickering light

## Complete Narrator Script

> You've been told a lie your entire life. Mirrors don't reflect light. They're windows to a parallel universe, and the thing staring back isn't you. It's an entity that has studied you since birth, perfectly mimicking every blink, every breath, every move. Why? Because it's guarding something. The mirror isn't a reflection. It's a wall, and the mimic is its warden. As long as you believe you're looking at yourself, you never question it. You never reach out. You never cross. But tonight, your reflection was a half-second late. Now it's smiling. And you never smiled.
