# Production Storyboard & Shot List

**Platform**: TikTok (9:16 Vertical)
**Generation Mode**: Text to Image
**Estimated Duration**: ~8s
**Total Scenes**: 1 | **Total Visual Beats**: 6

## Visual Style Profile
- **Art Style**: 2D vector-based chibi animation with smooth cel-shaded rendering, soft rounded silhouettes, and thick clean outlines.
- **Color Palette**: deep indigo twilight, neon kanji pink, warm steam amber, and mossy stone gray.
- **Lighting**: low-key nocturnal lighting with sharp highlights from paper lanterns and a soft volumetric glow radiating from the steaming broth.
- **Era & Setting**: anachronistic contemporary Tokyo during a rainy midnight at a secluded intersection marked by a weather-worn Shinto shrine gate.
- **Lens & Film Stock**: 35mm digital equivalent with a 24mm prime lens feel, shallow depth of field to isolate the stall against the blurred bokeh cityscape.

## Character Continuity Sheet
- **Ramen Chef**: Short chibi stature, large gentle eyes, wearing a indigo happi coat with a white apron and a traditional hachimaki headband tied neatly
- **Spirit Patron**: Translucent blue-tinted humanoid form with soft glowing edges and wispy, floaty aesthetic, wearing traditional tattered kimono layers
- **Wandering Ghost**: Small spectral chibi figure, semi-transparent, wearing a faded straw hat and simple peasant robes, carrying a small ethereal lantern

## Location Continuity Sheet
- **Midnight Ramen Stall**: A compact wooden yatai cart featuring a glowing red lantern, a steaming oversized iron cauldron, two small stools, and a hand-painted fabric curtain curtain fluttering in the breeze.
- **Forgotten Tokyo Crossroads**: Damp asphalt reflecting neon lights, moss-covered brick walls, a rusted metal street lamp, and a weather-beaten wooden shrine gate entrance.

## Complete Narrator Script

> A ramen chef operates a midnight street stall at a forgotten Tokyo crossroads that only spirits and wandering ghosts can see, serving warm broth that restores mortal memories.
