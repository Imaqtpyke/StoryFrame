# Production Storyboard & Shot List

**Platform**: TikTok (9:16 Vertical)
**Generation Mode**: Text to Image
**Estimated Duration**: ~22.7s

## Visual Style Profile
- **Art Style**: Authentic 1960s archival documentary realism with natural tactile textures, period-accurate NASA and MIT instrumentation interiors, warm film grain, vintage CRT and Nixie displays, and stacked paper computer listings
- **Color Palette**: Muted mid-century office tones of warm cream, olive green, charcoal gray, weathered wood paneling, pale green-bar computer paper, and subtle amber console glow against deep lunar charcoal and bright silver-white
- **Lighting**: Restrained dramatic documentary lighting balancing 3400K warm incandescent desk lamps with 5200K overhead office fluorescent fixtures, shifting to stark unfiltered 5600K solar rim light in outer space
- **Era & Setting**: 1969, Cambridge and Houston NASA engineering offices transitioning to Apollo 11 lunar orbit and the Moon surface
- **Lens & Film Stock**: Shot on 35mm prime lenses with authentic 1960s 16mm and 35mm archival documentary film grain

## Character Continuity Sheet
- **LeadSoftwareEngineer**: Mid-1960s American woman in her early thirties with shoulder-length dark brown wavy hair parted neatly, pale skin with light freckles, tortoise-shell rectangular eyeglasses, wearing a crisp cream collared blouse tucked into a charcoal high-waisted pleated midi skirt, a tailored muted olive cardigan, and low-heeled black leather pumps.
- **LunarAstronauts**: Two Apollo-era astronauts wearing white pressurized A7L lunar space suits with bulky life-support backpacks, ribbed joints, American flag shoulder patches, reflective gold-tinted helmet visors, and heavy lunar overshoes.

## Location Continuity Sheet
- **NASAInstrumentationOffice**: 1960s NASA and MIT Instrumentation Laboratory office featuring warm wood-paneled walls, steel filing cabinets, beige mainframe consoles with silver toggle switches and amber indicator lamps, long drafting tables stacked with green-bar fanfold computer code printouts and perforated punch cards, speckled linoleum floor, and overhead fluorescent tubes.
- **LunarOrbitAndSurface**: Stark airless lunar landscape of fine gray regolith dust, jagged impact craters, sunlit highland ridges, and a pitch-black starless void sky with the distant blue-and-white Earth hanging above the horizon.

## Complete Narrator Script

> In 1969, American engineer Margaret Hamilton leads NASA’s Apollo guidance-software team as Apollo 11 approaches the Moon. Facing last-minute computer alarms and a risky lunar landing, she trusts the code she built. On July 20, 1969, her work helps Neil Armstrong and Buzz Aldrin land safely on the Moon.
