# Production Storyboard & Shot List

**Platform**: TikTok (9:16 Vertical)
**Generation Mode**: Text to Image
**Estimated Duration**: ~14.1s
**Total Scenes**: 2 | **Total Visual Beats**: 9

## Visual Style Profile
- **Art Style**: Warm steampunk storybook animation with soft painterly textures, expressive character designs, intricate brass machinery, floating sky-islands, and glowing blue time-portal transitions between childhood memories and the present.
- **Color Palette**: Rich burnished brass, warm amber, copper-red, deep teal, weathered walnut brown, sunlit ivory clouds, and luminous cerulean blue temporal energy.
- **Lighting**: Warm 3200K amber workshop lantern and tungsten key lighting contrasted against cool 5400K to 5600K glowing blue portal and time-machine rim highlights.
- **Era & Setting**: Steampunk fantasy era in the floating sky-city of Virelia, centered around sky-bridges, clocktowers, and an heirloom timber-and-brass clockmaker workshop.
- **Lens & Film Stock**: Warm storybook cinema prime lenses with soft painterly atmospheric depth and subtle filmic texture.

## Character Continuity Sheet
- **Adult Juno**: Focused adult female clockmaker with copper-red hair, round brass goggles resting on her forehead, a dark teal work apron over a cream rolled-sleeve shirt, a pocketful of tiny brass gears, and sturdy brown leather workshop boots
- **Adult Milo**: Restless adult male inventor with tousled black hair, pale soot smudges on his hands, a patched brown leather-trimmed coat, a glowing brass-and-blue key pendant around his neck, dark trousers, and worn leather boots
- **Young Juno**: Child Juno with messy copper-red hair, a smudge of dark grease on her cheek, oversized faded overalls over a striped shirt, and small scuffed boots
- **Young Milo**: Child Milo with tousled black hair, a patched linen tunic and rolled trousers, small scuffed boots, carrying a small carved wooden toy bird

## Location Continuity Sheet
- **Floating City of Virelia**: Steampunk metropolis built atop drifting rocky sky-islands suspended among sunlit clouds, linked by brass suspension bridges, spinning windmills, copper clocktowers, and mooring docks.
- **Clockwork Workshop**: Warm amber-lit timber and stone workshop filled with tall oak tool shelves, hanging brass lanterns, scarred wooden workbenches, precision calipers, and suspended clockwork gears.

## Complete Narrator Script

> In the floating city of Virelia, clockmaker Juno discovers that her estranged brother Milo has been repairing forbidden time machines. Flashbacks show them as children building toys in their father’s workshop.
