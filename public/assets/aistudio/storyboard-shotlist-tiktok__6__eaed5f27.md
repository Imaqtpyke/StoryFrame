# Production Storyboard & Shot List

**Platform**: TikTok (9:16 Vertical)
**Generation Mode**: Text to Image
**Estimated Duration**: ~9s
**Total Scenes**: 1 | **Total Visual Beats**: 6

## Visual Style Profile
- **Art Style**: 3D stylized chibi rendering with soft rounded edges, matte tactile plastic textures for mechanical parts, and plush felt textures for nomad garments.
- **Color Palette**: Golden desert ochre, sun-bleached sandstone white, vibrant cobalt sky, and prismatic iridescent chrome highlights.
- **Lighting**: High-contrast harsh midday sun creating sharp, deep, yet soft-edged shadows, with intense lens flares reflecting off mirror surfaces.
- **Era & Setting**: Post-apocalyptic high-fantasy future, endless rolling sand dunes, scorching heat, cloudless zenith sky.
- **Lens & Film Stock**: 14mm ultra-wide prime lens for vertical immersion with a clean, grain-free digital crispness.

## Character Continuity Sheet
- **Nomad**: Chibi proportions with oversized head, hooded beige burlap cloak, brass-rimmed goggles over dark eyes, utility belt with glowing solar-charging nodes
- **Mechanical Beast**: Quadrupedal robot with oversized spherical joints, polished copper chassis, oversized light-bulb style solar panels on its back, rounded stubby limbs

## Location Continuity Sheet
- **The Dune Sea**: Infinite undulating mounds of golden sand, gentle slopes with rhythmic wind ripples, occasional half-buried ancient machinery.
- **Mirror Oasis**: A cluster of geometric floating quartz pillars and shattered mirrored glass pools that reflect the sky, ground surface made of shimmering crystallized sand.

## Complete Narrator Script

> A silent desert nomad guides a caravan of solar-powered mechanical beasts across an endless dune sea, seeking an oasis made entirely of crystallized mirrors.
