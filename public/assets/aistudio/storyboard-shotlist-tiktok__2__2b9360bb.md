## Complete Narrator Script

> If you deposit nine thousand nine hundred dollars in cash to avoid bank reporting rules, your bank is legally required to flag you for a federal crime. Under anti-money laundering laws, banks automatically file a Currency Transaction Report whenever someone deposits ten thousand dollars or more in cash. To bypass this, many people split large cash sums into smaller deposits like nine thousand eight hundred dollars, thinking they are staying under the radar. However, algorithm monitoring systems flag this exact behavior as "structuring," an intentional act to evade federal disclosures. The moment these repeated deposits hit your account, bank compliance officers must secretly submit a Suspicious Activity Report directly to federal investigators without notifying you or giving you a chance to explain, leading to frozen accounts or sudden federal asset seizures even if your money was completely legal.
